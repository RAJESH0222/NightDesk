WHY THIS SHAPE

      
    
        
  • Yahoo Finance via yfinance is UNOFFICIAL — no SLA, can break or rate-limit without warning. The sleeps in the script are deliberate; keep request volume low.
    • 
        
  • Finnhub free tier = 60 calls/min — plenty for a personal watchlist. Alpha Vantage's news free tier (25 requests/day) is deliberately avoided as too restrictive.
    • 
        
  • 15–20 min cadence is right for personal monitoring — near-real-time, not real-time. Between snapshots the frontend simulates small drift locally, clearly labelled.
    • 
        
  • Total cost: zero. Actions free tier + repo + Netlify free tier. Single user, no auth, nothing to patch.
